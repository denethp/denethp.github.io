01 — About

Designing systems that sense, decide, and move.

Robotics Advanced Control Embedded Systems Electronics Design Computer Vision Autonomous Systems PCB Design Mechanical Design

I'm an undergraduate at the University of Moratuwa reading for a B.Sc. (Hons) in Electronic and Telecommunication Engineering, currently ranked on the Dean's List with a CGPA of 3.83/4.0. My work sits at the intersection of robotics, control systems, embedded systems, and computer vision — I like taking a robot from a mechanical concept to a fully autonomous, closed-loop system.

Over the last two years, I've built robotic arms, advanced micromice with complex motion control systems, line-following and vision-guided autonomous robots, and a range of other embedded systems — while competing in national-level robotics competitions. I'm equally comfortable in SOLIDWORKS and Altium Designer as I am writing C/C++ firmware, developing control algorithms, or working with ROS 2.
